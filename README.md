## Machine Learning Field Guides

Visual, mathematical, and interview-ready references for topics I am learning
and revising. The guides build intuition from scratch, derive the core math,
trace worked examples, explain diagrams, and include the primary papers for
offline reading.

### [Open the complete field-guide library →](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/)

### Individual guides

- [Feature Interactions](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/ML%20-%20Feature%20Interactions/) — FM, Wide & Deep, DeepFM, xDeepFM, DCN v1/v2, AutoInt, DHEN, and Wukong.
- [Self-Attention & Multi-Head Attention](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/ML%20-%20Self%20Attention%20and%20Multi-Head%20Attention/) — embeddings, complete Q/K/V projections, scaled scores, masks, row-wise softmax, two worked heads, output projection, residuals, LayerNorm, and production tensor shapes.
- [FlashAttention](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/ML%20-%20FlashAttention/) — exact tiled attention, HBM versus SRAM traffic, online softmax, a complete two-tile numerical example, backward recomputation, and FlashAttention 1–4.
- [BatchNorm vs LayerNorm](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/ML%20-%20BatchNorm%20vs%20LayerNorm/) — normalization axes, a complete shared-matrix calculation, train/eval behavior, CNN and Transformer shapes, Pre-LN/Post-LN, and practical choices.
- [DCG, NDCG, AUC & Normalized Entropy](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/ML%20-%20Ranking%20Metrics/) — discounted gain, ideal-list normalization, a complete AUC pair/ROC calculation, production NE, calibration, aggregation, and metric pitfalls.
- [Activation Functions & SwiGLU](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/ML%20-%20Activation%20Functions%20and%20SwiGLU/) — sigmoid, tanh, ReLU, GELU, SiLU, gradient flow, Transformer residual context, and a complete numerical SwiGLU calculation.
- [HSTU](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/ML%20-%20HSTU/) — generative recommendation, a branch-by-branch HSTU layer, pointwise attention, a full vector example, M-FALCON candidate masking, and systems co-design.
- [Semantic IDs](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/ML%20-%20Semantic%20IDs/) — RQ-VAE, TIGER generative retrieval, and the discrete scaling bottleneck.
- [Sparse MoE (Shazeer et al.)](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/ML%20-%20Multimodal%20MMoE/#shazeer) — noisy top-k gating, conditional computation, expert load balancing, and a worked routing example.
- [Multimodal MMoE](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/ML%20-%20Multimodal%20MMoE/) — redundant, unique, and synergistic interactions with specialized experts and routing.
- [OneRec](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/ML%20-%20OneRec/) — unified retrieve-and-rank, balanced item tokens, sparse MoE, and iterative preference alignment.
- [PPO & GRPO](https://dhananjay014.github.io/htmls/RL%20-%20PPO%20and%20GRPO/) — policy gradients, advantage estimation, clipping, worked updates, and failure modes.
